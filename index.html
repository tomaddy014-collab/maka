How it works

Four steps to a
full class marked

From upload to printed feedback sheets in under two minutes per student.

1
Add your Gemini key
Paste your Gemini API key. For classroom use, use a key on a billing-enabled project — Google does not use paid-tier data to train its models.
2
Set your criteria
Choose learning area, curriculum level, and task. Use the default NZC rubric or write your own and save it for reuse.
3
Upload the class set
Drag and drop your whole class at once. PDFs, scanned JPGs, photos of handwriting — Gemini reads them all.
4
Get results instantly
Gemini marks, moderates its own scores, then rewrites every comment in your voice. Export CSV or print feedback sheets.
Features

Everything a NZ teacher
actually needs

Refreshed NZC aligned
Every score and comment references the refreshed New Zealand Curriculum. Levels 3–5 across all learning areas.
Two-pass marking
Pass 1 marks the work. Pass 2 moderates every score for fairness and consistency, then rewrites all feedback to sound like you — not an AI.

Consistent scoring
Always 5 criteria, always out of 25. No more students getting 13/20 while others get 9/15.
Your voice
Paste 3–5 of your own report comments. Every piece of feedback comes out sounding like you wrote it.
Class dashboard
Bands, averages per criterion, and a support flag for students averaging below 2.5/5.
Report & weekly comments
Generate full end-of-term report paragraphs or quick weekly posts for parents and whānau — all pulled from past marking sessions, all in your voice. Pronoun-aware (he/she/they) using your class list.
Private by design
Student PDFs go from your browser to your own Gemini API key, and results save to your own private Supabase storage. Use a billing-enabled Gemini key for marking: under Google’s paid-tier terms your data is not used to train their models (free-tier data can be). No other third party ever sees student work.
