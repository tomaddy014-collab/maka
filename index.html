How it works

Four steps to a
full class marked

From upload to printed feedback sheets in under two minutes per student.

1 🔑
Add your Gemini key
Paste your free Gemini API key. It saves to your account and stays in your browser — never sent anywhere else.
2 📋
Set your criteria
Choose learning area, curriculum level, and task. Use the default NZC rubric or write your own and save it for reuse.
3 📄
Upload the class set
Drag and drop your whole class at once. PDFs, scanned JPGs, photos of handwriting — Gemini reads them all.
4 ✨
Get results instantly
Gemini marks, moderates its own scores, then rewrites every comment in your voice. Export CSV or print feedback sheets.
